Aircraft Fuel Pumps are critical components of an aircraft's fuel system, responsible for delivering fuel from the tanks to the engines at the correct pressure and flow rate under various operating conditions.

The global Aircraft Fuel Pumps market is growing steadily, driven by increasing aircraft production and the need for fuel-efficient pump systems. In 2023, total unit sales reached 185,000, with commercial aviation accounting for 60% of demand. The military aircraft segment is growing at 6.5% annually. The market saw a 15% increase in demand for electric fuel pumps in 2023, reflecting the trend towards more electric aircraft systems.

Centrifugal pumps dominate with a 55% market share, but positive displacement pumps are growing at 7% annually due to their efficiency in handling low-flow, high-pressure applications. North America leads with a 40% market share, while Asia Pacific is the fastest-growing region at 7.2% CAGR. The industry is focusing on developing lightweight, high-efficiency pumps, with a 30% increase in R&D investments for composite materials and advanced pump designs.
